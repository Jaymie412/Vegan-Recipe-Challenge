Smoky Bean Chilli (serves 4)

Ingredients
- 2 tins kidney beans, drained
- 1 tin black beans, drained
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 250ml vegetable stock
- Salt and pepper

Method
1. Fry the onion, garlic and pepper in the olive oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add the spices, tomatoes, beans and stock.
3. Simmer for 30 minutes, season and serve with rice.
