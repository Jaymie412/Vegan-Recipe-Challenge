Creamy Tomato Pasta (serves 2)

Ingredients
- 200g spaghetti
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp butter
- 100ml double cream
- 30g grated parmesan
- Fresh basil

Method
1. Cook the spaghetti.
2. Fry the garlic in the oil, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the butter and cream, toss with the pasta and top with parmesan and basil.
